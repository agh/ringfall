# Ringfall glossary

> [Ringfall](../README.md) > [Specification](spec.md) > Glossary

These are the words the code, the documents, the issues, and the reviews
use, in alphabetical order. Each entry says what the word means in this
project and which file owns the concept, so a change to the meaning is
made there and not here.

**Abyss.** The darkest palette token, `#0b1a2e`, used for the sky zenith,
the page background, and the clear colour. The other tokens are Beacon
(`#7dd3fc`, the HUD accent, reticle, trims, and shield), Flare
(`#fbbf24`, the player's own state), Dawn (`#f6b26b`, the sun and horizon
glow), Ring (`#b8d8ea`, the ringworld arc and secondary text), Bone
(`#e8e4d8`, body text), Rose (`#fb7185`, damage and enemy emissive), Hull
(`#101f36`, panel fill), and Slate (`#8aa0b8`, muted text and borders).
The palette and its contrast ratios are owned by [brand.md](brand.md).

**Arena.** The 60 m square the player defends, with transparent boundary
walls and sixteen seeded cover blocks, none within 6 m of the centre.
Generated by `generateArena` in `src/core/world.ts` and explained in
[design-doc.md](design-doc.md).

**Bolt.** A strider's projectile: it travels at 22 m/s for up to 3 s,
stops on cover, the floor, and the arena edge, and hits a 0.9 m sphere
around the player's centre. Owned by `src/core/projectile.ts`.

**Bot.** The scripted player in `src/core/bot.ts` that reads the same
state a human sees and emits the same `InputFrame` a keyboard and mouse
would, with a bounded turn rate and aim jitter. It comes in a steady
profile that retreats at 30 percent shield and a reckless one that never
retreats. Its purpose is described in [test-plan.md](test-plan.md).

**Chapter 1.** The demo as specified in [spec.md](spec.md): one arena,
one rifle, four grenades, two enemies, five named waves, and a victory tableau,
lasting five to ten minutes. Anything beyond it is Later.

**Cover slab.** One of the sixteen cover blocks in the arena, 2 to 6 m in
footprint and 1.5 to 4 m tall, with a Beacon trim along its top edge. The
geometry is in `src/core/world.ts`; the trim rule is in
[brand.md](brand.md).

**Datum.** The shared horizontal line, the top edge of every cover slab,
on which every emissive trim strip sits, so that all the blocks read as
made by the same people. Defined in [brand.md](brand.md) under "Trim
conventions".

**Deploy.** The single button on the title overlay and the moment it
creates: click, pointer lock, HUD elements sliding in with a stagger, the
shield-recharge sting, and the combat bus armed. The choreography is
specified in [brand.md](brand.md) and story S2 of
[requirements.md](product/requirements.md); the handler is `start()` in
`src/main.ts`.

**Drop pod.** How every enemy arrives: `spawnWave` in `src/core/game.ts`
schedules a `PendingSpawn` per roster entry and, when its delay is up, the
enemy appears at its landing point and the `dropPod` event fires with the
enemy's id, kind, landing point and `activeAt`, 1.5 s (`DROP_POD_LEAD`)
before it can act. The renderer draws the streak and the dust burst.

**Feel pass.** The custom full-screen post-processing pass that adds the
vignette, chromatic aberration that grows with damage, the hexagonal
shield lattice that flares on hits, and grain, after bloom and before ACES
tone mapping. Owned by `src/render/post.ts` and described in
[design-doc.md](design-doc.md).

**GameEvent.** One entry in the list a simulation step returns to say what
happened during it: a shot, a reload, a grenade thrown, an explosion, an
enemy killed, player damage, a shield break, a bolt fired, a wave started
or cleared, the player dying, or victory. The renderer, HUD, audio bank,
and playtest harness each switch on them. The type lives in
`src/core/game.ts`.

**InputFrame.** One frame of player intent: forward and strafe from -1 to
1, the jump, sprint, fire, reload, and grenade flags, and absolute yaw and
pitch in radians. `src/input/input.ts` builds it from the keyboard and
mouse, and `src/core/bot.ts` builds it from state; the type lives in
`src/core/game.ts`.

**Later.** The explicit deferral list in [spec.md](spec.md), mirrored by
the Later epic in issue #3: melee, a second weapon, a third enemy, mobile
and WebGPU, and vehicles or a campaign. A feature that adds content
without changing one of the three loops is Later.

**MLP.** The Minimum Lovable Product: the fifteen numbered items in
[spec.md](spec.md), each named after the moment a first-time player would
describe to a friend, and mirrored by the MLP epic in issue #2.

**Playtest.** A whole game played by the bot in Node at 60 Hz with no
renderer, through `runPlaytest()` in `src/core/playtest.ts`, and the layer
of [test-plan.md](test-plan.md) that this produces. Its outputs are
defined in [metrics.md](metrics.md).

**Record mode.** The page state entered with `?record&bot&seed=N`, in
which the animation loop is suspended and `window.ringfallCapture` lets a
script step the simulation and render exactly once per call, so a film is
frame-exact whatever the GPU speed. Implemented in `src/main.ts` and used
by `scripts/record-demo.mjs`; see [runbook.md](runbook.md).

**Ring.** The ringworld the arena stands on: a torus 6 km in radius with a
360 m tube, sunk 820 m below the horizon so the arc rises from both sides
and crosses overhead, with a procedural ocean, land, and cloud texture on
its inner face. Built in `src/render/environment.ts` and justified in
[brand.md](brand.md); also the name of the palette token used to paint it.

**Seed.** The integer from which `createRng` in `src/core/rng.ts` derives
every random choice in a run, so that the arena layout and enemy jitter
are reproducible. Production seeds from the clock; tests, the bot, the
smoke test, and the recorder fix it, and the demo reel uses seed 7.

**Skimmer.** The fast melee enemy: 40 health, 7 m/s, a 12 damage zap
every 0.9 s, closing to 2 m with lateral jitter so packs fan out, worth
100 points. Its stats are in `src/core/enemy.ts` and its silhouette rules
in [brand.md](brand.md).

**Step.** One call to `stepGame(state, input, dt, rng)` with `dt` fixed at
1/60 s, returning the next immutable state and its events. The loop in
`src/main.ts` banks frame time and runs at most five steps per frame; the
harness and the recorder run steps directly. The rule is
[ADR-0002](adr/0002-pure-simulation-core.md).

**Strider.** The ranged enemy: 120 health, 3 m/s, a bolt for 18 damage
every 1.4 s, holding 8 to 16 m and backing off when crowded, worth 300
points. Its stats are in `src/core/enemy.ts`.

**Tableau.** The end of a won run: when the Last Stand's armoured leader
dies, `victory` fires with kills and shield breaks, `GameState.tableau`
holds for `TABLEAU_DURATION` (5 s) with input frozen and the player safe
while the survivors flee off the arena, and then the status turns `won`.
`timeScaleOf(state)` asks for 0.2x speed for its first 1.5 s of wall clock.

**Trim.** A thin emissive strip in Beacon on world geometry, never more
than 5 percent of an object's surface and always on the datum. The rule is
in [brand.md](brand.md) and the strips are built in `src/render/`.

**Vitality.** The player's shield over health model: 100 points of shield
absorbed first, 100 points of health second, the shield recharging at 35
per second after 4 s without damage and health never recharging. Owned by
`src/core/vitality.ts`.

**Wave phases.** The four states of `WaveState.phase` in
`src/core/waves.ts`: `countdown` for 3 s, `active` until every enemy is
dead, `cleared` for a 2.5 s pause, and `won` after wave 5, the value of
`FINAL_WAVE`, whose armoured leader's death ends the run. The music follows the phase: combat while active with
enemies alive, calm otherwise.
